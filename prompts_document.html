Platform Executive Summary & Problem Statement

Core Concept: Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) face massive price disadvantages when procuring industrial raw materials (metals, polymers, construction steel, solvents) compared to large corporations who access deep Tier-1 factory discounts. Synapse B2B solves this by aggregating raw material demand through Pooled Co-Procurement ("Buy Together to Cut Costs"), unlocking volume pricing up to 25% below open market rates. Built with an authentic Tiffany Blue (#0abab5) light theme, verified Mill Test Certificates (MTC 3.1), Incoterms logistics pricing, and formal digital PO generation.
